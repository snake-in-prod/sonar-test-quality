A widget test runs on a fake clock. Awaiting Future.delayed or calling sleep in the test body waits for real time instead of moving that clock.

Why is this an issue?

Under the fake clock the delay never completes until something pumps, so the test hangs or times out; under a live binding it makes the suite slow and the result depends on the machine. tester.pump(duration) moves the fake clock exactly; real waits that are needed belong in tester.runAsync.

Noncompliant code example

await tester.pumpWidget(const MyApp());
await Future.delayed(const Duration(seconds: 1)); // Noncompliant
expect(find.text('Home'), findsOneWidget);

Compliant solution

await tester.pumpWidget(const MyApp());
await tester.pump(const Duration(seconds: 1));
expect(find.text('Home'), findsOneWidget);
